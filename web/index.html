LANCEDECK

A free, unofficial team tracker for MechWarrior Online. It reads your own screen, nothing else: no memory reading, no input, no network calls to the game. Everything it records stays on this computer.

Mech and map pictures are imported from your own game install and never redistributed. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Piranha Games Inc. MechWarrior and BattleTech are trademarks of their owners.

Free software under the GNU GPL v3: use it, share it, improve it; anyone who distributes it must share the source the same way.

Lance emblems by Lorc and Delapouite from game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0.

If it helps you win, a donation keeps it maintained through the game's patches.

New builds are published on GitHub. The only outbound call this app ever makes is the update check, and only when you press the button.
